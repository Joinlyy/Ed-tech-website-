# Customers

## Persona 1 — The Class-12 parent

**Name-shorthand:** *Anjali, 44, Pune.* Working professional, one child in Class 12 CBSE
Science. Already paying for two tuitions.

**Goal.** Know, honestly, whether the money and time going into tuition is producing marks.
Not "attendance", not "he's improving" — actual marks against the board pattern.

**Frustration.** School PTMs say the child is "doing well". Coaching classes send bar charts.
No one hands her a script and says: *here are the 14 marks he lost this week, here's the
one habit he needs to stop.*

**Job-to-be-done.** *When my child is three months from boards, help me focus our remaining
time on the one thing that actually moves the score.*

## Persona 2 — The Class-10 student

**Name-shorthand:** *Kabir, 15, Ghaziabad.* First serious board year. Anxious. Studies
hard but freezes in three-hour papers.

**Goal.** Build the stamina and habit of writing full papers, and find out which chapters
are shaky *before* the board itself finds out.

**Frustration.** Practice tests are shorter than boards, and the marking is loose. He can't
tell if he's getting better or worse over time because every test is graded by a different
teacher with different standards.

**Job-to-be-done.** *Give me five consistent, board-standard papers across the year so I
know if I'm actually improving.*

## Persona 3 — The retired examiner (staff persona)

**Name-shorthand:** *Prof. Mehta, 62, Bengaluru.* Retired CBSE evaluator. Wants meaningful
part-time work, dislikes the rushed, uneven marking that happens during official evaluation
season.

**Goal.** Mark carefully, with time to explain, at a fair per-paper rate.

**Frustration.** Existing edtech "expert" gigs push volume over care, and there's no
software helping with the mechanical parts (matching to the marking scheme, chasing
missing units, tabulating totals).

**Job-to-be-done.** *Let me focus on judgment. Handle the mechanical bits before the paper
reaches me.*
