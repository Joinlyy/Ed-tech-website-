# Product Overview

## Vision

Every mark a student loses on a CBSE board paper should have a name, a location on the page,
and a fix a parent can act on tonight. Right now, most families see only a total. RedPen makes
the invisible marks visible.

## Problem statement

Board-exam prep in India is optimised for volume (more topics, more mocks, more tuitions) and
not for accuracy of feedback. When a Class 12 student scores 68/100 in a mock, no one on the
supply side is incentivised to explain *which* 32 marks went missing and *why*. Schools mark for
speed, coaching classes mark for effect, and the child re-reads the same chapter without
knowing that the real problem was skipping two lines of working in every derivation.

The consequence is expensive: parents fund tuitions that don't address the actual gap, and
students walk into the boards with a false sense of readiness.

## One-sentence product definition

RedPen is a subscription service that marks CBSE Class 10 and 12 board-pattern papers by
hand — first pass by software, second pass by an experienced CBSE evaluator — and returns an
annotated script plus a one-page report that names every mark lost and the single habit
costing the student the most.

## What we are not

- Not a tuition provider. We do not teach.
- Not an AI grader alone — every mark is human-reviewed before release.
- Not a rank generator — we never compare a student to any other student.
- Not affiliated with CBSE or any examination board.
